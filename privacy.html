Privacy Policy — Pawline Uploader

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What this app is

Pawline Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the Pawline Picks YouTube channel. It uploads the owner's own original videos to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. The app is not available to the public and has no other users.

Data the app accesses

With the channel owner's authorization, the app uses the YouTube youtube.upload permission only. It accesses the authorization token granted by the owner and the video files the owner provides for upload. It does not read, collect, or store personal information about any other person.

How data is used

Authorization tokens are used solely to authenticate uploads to the owner's channel. Video files are transmitted to YouTube for publication. No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data storage and retention

Authorization tokens are stored in secure credential storage and are used only for the uploads described above. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page, which immediately ends the app's access.
