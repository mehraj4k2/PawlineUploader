Pawline Uploader

Pawline Uploader is a private, single-user tool used by the owner of the Pawline Picks YouTube channel to upload their own original videos to their own channel through the official YouTube Data API.

The tool is not offered to the public. It has no other users, accounts, or sign-up. It requests only the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) so finished videos can be published to the owner's channel on a schedule.
